From idea to website

Start with a conversation.

No instant checkout. No complicated pitch. Just tell me what you want to make, and give me a clear place to start.

  1. 01

    Show me your idea

    Tell me who the site is for, what it needs to do, and the address you would like.

  2. 02

    Bring good references

    Share website links, screenshots, or a moodboard. Explain what you like about them, and include your text and images if you have them.

  3. 03

    Agree on the project

    I’ll decide whether I can take it on. We discuss the design, scope, any paid extras, and how the website will be hosted.

  4. 04

    Build, review, share

    If we go ahead, we work through the website together before it is ready to share.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Is a website always free?

No. I may take on a project for free when it interests me and I have time. Paid work and extras are discussed before anything is agreed. Sending a request doesn’t commit either of us.

What should I send with my request?

A short introduction, your website’s purpose, a few useful references, the pages you need, and any text, images, or links you already have. Tell me what you like about each reference.

Do I get my own domain name?

The usual address is a subdomain, such as yourname.creatorpromote.com. That is different from owning a separate domain such as yourname.com. A custom domain can be discussed separately.

Can I choose any name?

Suggest a name in your request. I’ll check whether it is available and suitable when we discuss the project. The brief builder only previews your suggestion; it does not check or reserve it.

What about changes after the site is built?

Tell me if you need updates, maintenance, or help managing the content. We agree on how that will work and any cost for your project. There is no automatic package or promised response time.
